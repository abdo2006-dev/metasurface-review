## D. Load-bearing claims (verbatim from `manuscript/01_introduction.md` §1.6)

The manuscript's four contributions, quoted rather than paraphrased so their evidence framing is not accidentally strengthened in the retelling:

> **C1 — Architecture-aware adaptation-chain framework.** FIM adaptation is decomposed into ten stages from sensing to stabilised radiation, and each source's treatment of each stage is coded on two axes: what the source establishes about the stage, and what it establishes about how long the stage takes. Applied to the reviewed set, no platform measures the duration of more than four of the ten stages, and the platform reaching four has no radio-frequency layer.
>
> **C2 — Definition-preserving timing register.** Every reported value is recorded with its timed object, start event, end event and measurement status, and values may be compared only within a comparability class. Two common simplifications fail that test: an element-level update time cannot stand in for an aperture-level configuration time, as one panel's own two bounds show; and the reported ranges for electronic and mechanical processes overlap once the tuning mechanism is named, so an architecture label alone does not order them.
>
> **C3 — Traceability audit of the hardware-feasibility premises.** Three premises from the FIM system literature are traced to the primary measurements behind them. Among the 13 of 20 candidate citing studies whose full texts were accessible, four reuse a timing value with its scope changed, and those four form one connected co-authorship network; the remainder cite the same hardware and attach no timing value at all. Every count is a lower bound on a partially observed set, and no prevalence claim is made. Section 5.2 gives the instances and the bounds.
>
> **C4 — Validation gap and reporting set.** Four intervals are unmeasured in the reviewed set, and the reporting fields we propose prescribe definitions — object, start event, end event — rather than acceptance thresholds.

**Evidence classification: C3 is held at Level A** in `documentation/novelty_boundary.md` and `documentation/evidence_strength_matrix.md` — a documented pattern within one traced collaboration network, on **zero author-disjoint confirming groups**, with 7 of the 20 identified system studies still unread behind paywalls. If a later, better-resourced search finds an author-disjoint instance, the classification moves to Level B; that is the single open item that could change a contribution's wording. Reviewers should read `manuscript/05_validation_gap.md` §5.2, `documentation/supplement_propagation.md` (Supplementary Section S8) and `documentation/audits/independence_audit.md` directly rather than relying on this summary for anything load-bearing.

## E. Two things that are load-bearing and must not drift

**C1 must name its axis.** Three counts exist and they differ: quantitatively evidenced stages (maximum **6**, on two platforms), measured stage durations (maximum **4**, on BAI-22 alone, which does not radiate), and stages carrying any duration information (maximum **6**, on LI-25 alone). Any restatement of C1 that does not say which of the three it means is wrong. All three are recomputed from `documentation/adaptation_chain_matrix.md` by `tools/c1_stage_counts.py`.

**C3 is stated within its observation window.** 13 of 20 candidate studies were read; of the seven unread, exactly one is author-disjoint from both the lineage and all three seeds, and that one study is the whole residual risk to the independence finding.

## F. Known limitations

- **No domain-expert review has occurred.** The largest single gap; this project cannot close it by itself.
- **No interval in the manuscript carries an uncertainty.** No reviewed source reports a repetition count or dispersion figure for its headline timing value.
- **7 of the 20 identified system studies are behind publisher paywalls and were not read.** Every count from the forward-citation search is a lower bound; an author-disjoint instance among them would move C3 from Level A to Level B.
- **Some sources are identified bibliographically only, and one is read at abstract level.** The bibliography itself carries no access annotations; per-source access status is in [`SOURCE_VERSION_MAP.md`](SOURCE_VERSION_MAP.md), which states for every entry whether the full text was obtained and whether the version of record was read. No load-bearing quantitative claim rests on a source that was not read in full.
- **The documented external search returned one confident false negative.** It concluded that no FIM-dedicated overview existed; one had been public as a preprint for five months, and it is now cited in §1. The search that found it, and the search that tested the corpus boundary against morphing and 4D-printed radiating apertures, are recorded as S12 and S13 in [`documentation/literature_search_log.md`](documentation/literature_search_log.md). Neither is exhaustive. Absence claim A3 was withdrawn as a result ([`documentation/absence_claims.md`](documentation/absence_claims.md)).
